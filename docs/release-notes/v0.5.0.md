# PDFE v0.5.0

A feature-and-bug-fix release over v0.4.3, rolling up the image/stamp
interaction fixes, a 23-item defect sweep, and two signing enhancements. No
on-disk format changes; a drop-in update.

## Highlights

- **Sign a document without supplying your own certificate.** The Sign dialog can
  now generate a self-signed certificate on the spot — it creates an RSA key and
  a self-signed X.509 certificate and packages them as a passphrase-protected
  PKCS#12, then signs with it exactly as it would with a user-supplied `.pfx`.
  (GitHub #361)
- **Reuse signature images across sessions.** A browsed signature image can be
  saved to a named library and re-selected in a later session, instead of being
  used once and forgotten. (GitHub #362)

## Placed-image editing

- Placed images can be moved again, rotation direction matches the toolbar/command
  convention, and rotated images remain selectable by their rotated handles.
  (GitHub #340, #341, #342)

## Pages

- Fixed crashes and page-tree corruption around cross-page drag, and the
  page-reorder drop target is now a clear horizontal insertion line rather than a
  vertical bar. (GitHub #329 follow-up, #343, #344, #345)
- Thumbnails refresh on structural undo/redo and on inserting an image.
  (GitHub #346, #347, #348)
- Crop honors the page coordinate (Y-flip) correctly, grayscale conversion is
  undoable, and the watermark dialog's text mode works as intended.
  (GitHub #354, #355, #356)

## Measure, Compare, and markup

- Distance measurements commit their real endpoints; Perimeter/Area measurements
  are discoverable and complete cleanly. (GitHub #349, #350)
- Compare honors the chosen highlight color, aligns the overlay to scale, clamps
  navigation at the ends, and draws a translucent one-sided difference marker.
  (GitHub #351, #352, #353)
- Region-rectangle text highlight falls back correctly. (GitHub #367)

## File, toolbar, and UI

- Combine-files drag reorder and auto-open behave correctly, and a failed
  image-stamp placement now fails loudly instead of silently. (GitHub #357, #358,
  #359)
- Customize-Toolbars preserves the current layout, Help links point at the public
  project URLs, and sticky-tool behavior is fixed. (GitHub #363, #364, #365, #366)

## Signing robustness

- The signature field is rolled back when signing is cancelled, and a
  use-after-free on the sign-then-reopen path is fixed. (GitHub #360)

## Tracking

- Filed a canonical tracker for three environment-only `FzTryRaiiBoundary`
  static-analysis test failures (a stale ASan compile database), for parity with
  the sibling environment-only item. (GitHub #368)

Full detail: `docs/audit/many-bugs-2026-11-closing.md`,
